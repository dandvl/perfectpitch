# Perfect Pitch

An Android app designed to help musicians and music enthusiasts develop their ability to identify musical notes by ear 
- a skill known as "perfect pitch" or "absolute pitch".

## 🎵 About

Perfect Pitch is an interactive training app that plays musical notes and challenges users to identify them correctly. 
The app includes all 12 notes of the chromatic scale and provides immediate feedback to help users improve their musical ear training.

## ✨ Features

- **Note Identification Training**: Listen to randomly selected musical notes and identify them from multiple-choice options
- **Complete Chromatic Scale**: Practice with all 12 notes including sharps and flats
- **Score Tracking**: Keep track of your current score and best score across sessions
- **Immediate Feedback**: Get instant feedback on whether your answer was correct or incorrect
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Each session consists of 10 rounds to test your skills
- **Clean UI**: Modern Material Design 3 interface with smooth animations
- **Persistent Scores**: Your best score is saved locally and persists between app sessions

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Kotlin**
- **Jetpack Compose**
- **MVI**
- **Coroutines**
- **Material Design 3**: Design system
- **MockK**, **JUnit**

## 📱 Requirements

- **Android SDK**: Minimum SDK 29 (Android 10.0)
